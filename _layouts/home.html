How We Work

Engagement Process

Every engagement follows a clear four-phase structure — from kickoff to actionable findings. Timelines scale with scope.

Week 1 · ~5 days

Scoping & Kickoff

We define engagement boundaries, target scope, rules of engagement, and success criteria. A written scope agreement is signed before any technical work begins. Stakeholders align on timelines, communication channels, and escalation paths.

  • Written scope & rules of engagement
  • Target inventory & out-of-scope list
  • Kickoff call with technical stakeholders
  • Emergency contact & escalation paths
Week 2 · ~7 days

Reconnaissance & Discovery

Passive and active reconnaissance to map your attack surface. We identify services, technologies, entry points, and likely weaknesses before any exploitation. For AI engagements, this includes model surface mapping and data flow analysis.

  • External & internal attack surface mapping
  • Authentication & authorization review
  • Technology stack & version enumeration
  • Weekly progress update delivered
Week 3 · ~5 days

Exploitation & Validation

We validate findings with controlled, authorized exploitation to prove real impact — not theoretical risk. Every action is logged; nothing is done that is outside the written scope. Critical findings are reported immediately, not held for the final report.

  • Proof-of-concept development
  • Lateral movement & privilege escalation
  • Real-time critical-finding disclosure
  • Detection & response gap analysis
Week 4 · ~5 days

Reporting & Remediation

You receive an executive summary for leadership and a technical report for engineering — prioritized by CVSS severity, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, with step-by-step remediation guidance. A live readout walks through findings; a retest is included within 30 days.

  • Executive & technical report
  • CVSS severity & MITRE ATT&CK mapping
  • Live findings readout call
  • Free retest within 30 days
